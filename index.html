Day Risk Planner

Simulates one slate of the bets from Portfolio Kelly. Stakes are scaled down until the 95% worst case fits inside your daily loss budget.

The bar on 0 is every day the wins and losses cancel. A day at or above break-even is common, but a profit on every day is not possible without arbitrage.

Line Movement & Closing Line Value

Log the price you got and the price at close. If you keep beating the close, your bets are being priced better than the market's final view, which is the best early sign of a real edge. Add the other side's closing price to remove the vig from the close.

Log at least two bets to see the running average.

BetOpenYoursCloseLine moveCLV

* No closing price for the other side was entered, so the close still includes the book's margin and CLV reads lower than it really is.

CLV is a leading indicator, not a result. It takes roughly 100 or more bets before a positive average means much.

Bankroll Guardrails

Hard limits that apply to bets you log from the slate, and a warning for bets you add by hand. Losses count settled losses plus money still open today or this week. Set a limit to 0 to switch it off. After a drawdown from your peak bankroll, slate stakes are cut automatically.

Promo & Bonus-Bet Converter

Turns a sportsbook promo into locked cash by hedging the other side at a different book or a prediction market. This is one of the few structurally positive-expectation plays: you are converting a gift, not predicting a game. Prediction-market fees and book limits are not modeled; treat results as an estimate.
